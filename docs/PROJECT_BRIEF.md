# Project Brief — Remas Land

## Purpose
**Remas Land** is an English-only mobile e-commerce & wallet app (Android + iOS) for fresh artisanal Egyptian dairy, farm products, and pastries (milk, cheese, yogurt, butter & cream, Qashtouta desserts, ice cream, and bakery items), powered completely by a local SQLite backend (Drift) with no remote server.

## Core User Flow
1. **Browse Home (`Home`)**: View delivery address & notifications, swipe promo banners, browse "Explore Qishta & Co." aisles, "The Spotlight" products, "Special Offers", "Shop by Brands", "Shop Deals & More", and "Shop by Category" grid; quick-add items to cart.
2. **Search & Filter (`Search`)**: Search products by keyword, tap popular search chips (`Fresh Cheese`, `Milk`, `Qashtouta`, etc.), filter results, and add items to cart.
3. **Browse Category (`Category Products`)**: Drill into any category/brand/deal from Home or Empty Cart, search within category, filter (`All`, `Popular`, `New Arrivals`, `On Sale`), sort (`Recommended`, etc.), toggle favorites, and adjust cart quantities inline.
4. **Manage Cart (`Cart`)**:
   - **Empty Cart**: View empty-state illustration, tap "Explore Products" or trending category pills to jump back into discovery.
   - **Populated Cart**: Adjust item quantities or remove items, add "Goes Great With" pairings, view cold-chain guarantee, apply promo codes, review itemized bill, and proceed to checkout (local order placement).
5. **Wallet & Loyalty (`Pay`)**: View EGP balance (with hide/show toggle), top up balance or manage saved cards, view "Qishta Privilege" digital card & perks, access quick-pay actions (`Send Cash`, `Scan QR`, `Fawry Pay`, `Auto-Debit`), and inspect transaction history.
6. **Account & Care (`More`)**: View customer profile & loyalty tier, access `Orders` and `Rewards` bento cards, view `Morning Milking Pass`, manage favorites, invite a friend, promo codes, delivery addresses, concierge chat, settings, help/FAQs, and log out.
